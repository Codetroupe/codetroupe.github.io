Codetroupe Plugins portal

Privacy

Updated November 6, 2026

This notice covers the Codetroupe Plugins portal at the root of codetroupe.github.io. Individual plugin websites, plugins, and services have their own privacy notices and terms.

What happens in your browser

The portal stores an optional theme preference under codetroupe-portal-theme in your browser’s local storage. It records only System, Light, or Dark. You can change the preference in the Theme selector or remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Plugin search runs in your browser. Search text is not sent to a search service or saved by the portal. This portal does not include analytics scripts, advertising, an account system, or a payment form.

Website hosting

The portal is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web requests as the hosting provider, under its own policies. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Product websites and external services

Links may take you to a product website, JetBrains Marketplace, documentation, or another external service. Their own policies apply there. The theme setting for this portal is separate from product website preferences.

Questions

Use the product-specific options on our support page for questions about a plugin. Please avoid sending confidential project material or credentials.
