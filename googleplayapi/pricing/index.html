A few details

Before you purchase

Is there a free trial?

No trial period is planned. Use the public tutorials and integration guides to review the workflow and its requirements before purchase.

Will this be a subscription or a one-time purchase?

The final price and license scheme are still being prepared. The authorized purchase flow will state the license duration, seat entitlement, update rights and any renewal conditions. This page does not promise lifetime updates or a perpetual license.

Does the license include Google services?

The license covers this IDE plugin. Google Play, Firebase, Android dependencies, IDEs and service usage are governed separately by their providers. The generated subscription component is for purchases in your Android app; it does not purchase this plugin.

Does using generated code make my app open source?

No. You keep ownership of your project. The EULA permits commercial use of lawfully generated publisher-owned output without requiring you to publish your application source. Third-party terms still apply.
