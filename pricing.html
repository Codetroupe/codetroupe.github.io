Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ADB Pro pricing and licensing.

What happens after the trial?

After the 30-day trial, Personal features are locked until you subscribe. Free features —AAB Tools, Build Tools, and Signing Tools —remain available at no cost, so you can continue basic workflows without a subscription.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade anytime through the JetBrains Marketplace. Changes take effect immediately, and pricing is prorated for the remaining period.

Is there a perpetual license?

Yes. After 12 consecutive months of subscription, you receive a Perpetual Fallback License for the version you had at that time. This license never expires, even if you cancel your subscription.

Do you offer student discounts?

Pricing is managed through the JetBrains Marketplace. Check JetBrains student programs for available educational discounts and free licenses for students and teachers.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime through your JetBrains account. There are no cancellation fees. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.
